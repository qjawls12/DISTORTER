Credits and licences

The corpora the models were trained and evaluated on, the components they build upon, and what each licence allows.

Training data

EMO: A Paired Vocal Fry Scream Dataset
Ting-Chao Hsu, Music and AI Lab, National Taiwan University. Zenodo, 2026. doi:10.5281/zenodo.18356449. Licensed CC BY 4.0. Used to train the scream converter; the recordings were resampled and cropped, and no dataset audio is redistributed here.
EMVD: Extreme Metal Vocals Dataset
Modan Tailleur, Julien Pinquier, Laurent Millot, Corsin Vogel and Mathieu Lagrange. Zenodo, 2023. doi:10.5281/zenodo.8406322. Licensed CC BY 4.0. Its clean, HardcoreScream, DeathGrowl and BlackShriek recordings were used to train the three converters. One clear-voice take appears among the audio examples as the input to a conversion; no unmodified dataset audio is redistributed.

Evaluation data

CSTR VCTK Corpus (version 0.92)
Junichi Yamagishi, Christophe Veaux and Kirsten MacDonald. University of Edinburgh, Centre for Speech Technology Research, 2019. doi:10.7488/ds/2645. Licensed CC BY 4.0.
VocalSet: A Singing Voice Dataset
Julia Wilkins, Prem Seetharaman, Alison Wahl and Bryan Pardo. ISMIR 2018. Licensed CC BY 4.0. The spectrogram pair in the paper's inference figure, and two of the audio examples here, are held-out VocalSet excerpts.
GTSinger
Yu Zhang and others. NeurIPS 2024, Datasets and Benchmarks Track. Licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Used only to compute evaluation statistics. No GTSinger audio, and no derivative of it, is published on this site or in the paper.

Models and components

BigVGAN-v2
Sang-gil Lee, Wei Ping, Boris Ginsburg, Bryan Catanzaro and Sungroh Yoon, ICLR 2023. The official 24 kHz 100-band checkpoint is used unmodified for the look-ahead and offline renderers.
Vocos
Hubert Siuzdak, ICLR 2024. The low-latency renderer is a causal decoder in the Vocos style, trained for this work.
CycleGAN and CycleGAN-VC3
Jun-Yan Zhu, Taesung Park, Phillip Isola and Alexei A. Efros, ICCV 2017; Takuhiro Kaneko, Hirokazu Kameoka, Kou Tanaka and Nobukatsu Hojo, Interspeech 2020. The unpaired training setup and the discriminator lineage follow this work.
ConvNeXt
Zhuang Liu, Hanzi Mao, Chao-Yuan Wu, Christoph Feichtenhofer, Trevor Darrell and Saining Xie, CVPR 2022. The converter is a past-only 2D ConvNeXt U-Net.
